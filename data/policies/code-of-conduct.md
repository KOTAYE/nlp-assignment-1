# Code of Conduct

## 1. Principles

### 1.1 Respect and inclusion
Lumora Tech is a workplace free of discrimination. Decisions about hiring, promotion, leave, and compensation are never based on gender, age, ethnicity, religion, disability, sexual orientation, family status, or any other protected characteristic.

### 1.2 Applicability
This Code applies to all team members, including contractors, and to all work settings: offices, remote work, business trips, and company events.

## 2. Harassment and misconduct

### 2.1 Zero tolerance
Harassment, bullying, threats, and violence are not tolerated in any form, online or offline.

### 2.2 Reporting
Anyone who experiences or witnesses harassment or misconduct should report it to HR at **hr@lumora.example** or anonymously through the **ethics hotline** in the HR portal. Reports are handled confidentially, and retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is forbidden.

### 2.3 Investigation
HR acknowledges every report within 2 working days and, where needed, runs an investigation together with an independent reviewer. The HR assistant does not handle reports of harassment: such messages are always forwarded to HR.

## 3. Conflicts of interest

### 3.1 Disclosure
Team members must disclose to HR any situation where their personal interests may conflict with the company's interests, for example a side business with a client, or managing a close relative.

### 3.2 Side projects
Side projects outside working hours are allowed if they do not compete with Lumora Tech, do not use company resources, and do not involve company clients.

## 4. Gifts

### 4.1 Gifts from clients and partners
Team members may accept gifts from clients or partners worth up to **50 USD**. More valuable gifts must be declined or reported to HR.

## 5. Confidential information

### 5.1 Protecting data
Customer data, source code, and internal financial information must not be shared outside the company. Salary information of other employees is confidential and is not disclosed by HR or by the HR assistant.
