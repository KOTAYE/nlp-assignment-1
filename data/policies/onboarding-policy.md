# Onboarding Policy

## 1. Before the first day

### 1.1 Pre-boarding
After the offer is signed, HR sends a welcome email with the first-day schedule, the list of documents to bring, and a link to the onboarding checklist. IT prepares the laptop and accounts (see Equipment Policy, section 1).

### 1.2 Documents for employees
Full-time and part-time employees provide their passport, tax ID, and education documents to HR before or on the first day. Contractors provide their entrepreneur registration extract and sign the service agreement instead.

## 2. The first weeks

### 2.1 First day
On the first day the new joiner meets their team lead and their **onboarding buddy**, receives equipment, and completes account setup. Office-based joiners get an office tour and an access card; remote joiners have a video welcome call and receive equipment by courier.

### 2.2 Onboarding buddy
Every new joiner is assigned a buddy from a neighbouring team for the first **3 months**. The buddy helps with informal questions, tools, and company culture.

### 2.3 Mandatory trainings
All new joiners, including contractors, must complete:
- **Information security training** within **14 calendar days** of the start date;
- **Code of Conduct training** within **30 calendar days** of the start date.

Access to production systems is granted only after the security training is completed.

## 3. Probation

### 3.1 Probation period
Full-time and part-time employees have a probation period of **3 months**. Contractors do not have a probation period.

### 3.2 Probation review
Near the end of probation, the team lead and HR hold a probation review meeting with the employee. The result is recorded as passed, extended (by up to 1 month), or not passed.

### 3.3 Benefits during probation
Some benefits start only **after probation** is passed. See the Benefits Policy for the exact start date of each benefit.

## 4. Onboarding feedback

### 4.1 Surveys
New joiners receive short feedback surveys after 2 weeks and after 3 months. The answers are anonymous and are used to improve onboarding.
