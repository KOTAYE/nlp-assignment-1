# Leave Policy

## 1. Scope and eligibility

### 1.1 Purpose
This policy describes the types of leave available at Lumora Tech, how much leave employees are entitled to, and how leave is requested and approved. Sick leave is covered separately in the Sick Leave Policy.

### 1.2 Who is covered
This policy applies to all **full-time** and **part-time employees**. **Contractors** (individual entrepreneurs working under a service agreement) are **not covered** by this policy: their time off is governed by their service agreement, and they should agree on absences directly with their manager. Contractors cannot submit leave requests through the HR assistant.

### 1.3 Part-time employees
Part-time employees receive the same number of leave days as full-time employees. Leave pay for part-time employees is calculated in proportion to their contracted hours.

## 2. Annual leave

### 2.1 Entitlement
Eligible employees are entitled to **24 working days** of paid annual leave per calendar year. Weekends and company holidays (see section 6) that fall within a leave period are not deducted from the balance.

### 2.2 First year of employment
In the year an employee joins, the entitlement is **2 working days per full calendar month** remaining in that year, counted from the start date, and it is available from the first day. A month counts as full only if employment starts on its first day. For example, an employee who started on 1 September is entitled to 8 days for that year, and one who started on 15 September is entitled to 6 days (October–December).

### 2.3 Using annual leave
Annual leave can be taken in parts. At least one part of annual leave in a calendar year should be no shorter than 5 consecutive working days, so that employees can rest properly. This is a recommendation, not a condition for approval.

### 2.4 Leave balance
Employees can check their current balance at any time through the HR assistant. The balance shows the yearly entitlement, carried-over days, days already approved, and days remaining.

## 3. Requesting and approving annual leave

### 3.1 How to request
Annual leave is requested through the HR assistant by giving the start date and end date. The request is checked automatically against the rules below and receives one of three statuses: **approved**, **declined**, or **pending HR review**. A single request cannot span two calendar years; leave over New Year is submitted as two requests.

### 3.2 Notice period
- Leave of **more than 3 working days** must be requested at least **14 calendar days** before the start date.
- Leave of **up to 3 working days** must be requested at least **2 working days** before the start date.

Requests that do not meet the notice period are declined automatically. In urgent situations, employees should contact HR directly.

### 3.3 Automatic decision rules
A request for annual leave is:
- **approved** if the employee has enough remaining balance, the notice period is met, and the dates do not overlap with the employee's own pending or approved requests;
- **declined** if the balance is insufficient, the notice period is not met, the dates overlap with the employee's own existing request, or the employee is not eligible (see 1.2);
- sent for **HR review** if **two or more members of the same team** already have approved leave on any of the requested days. HR then confirms with the team lead and makes the final decision within 3 working days.

### 3.4 Carry-over
Up to **5 unused annual leave days** may be carried over to the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by **31 March** of that year; otherwise they expire. Days above the carry-over limit expire on 31 December.

> Note: The 2025 version of this policy allowed 10 carry-over days. The limit was reduced to 5 days from 1 January 2026.

### 3.5 Cancelling leave
An employee can cancel a pending or approved request through the HR assistant at any time before the leave starts. Cancelled days are returned to the balance. Leave that has already started cannot be cancelled by the employee; contact HR.

## 4. Special leave

### 4.1 Types of special leave
Employees are entitled to paid special leave for the following life events:

| Event | Days per event |
|---|---|
| Own wedding | 3 working days |
| Bereavement (death of a close family member) | 3 working days |
| Birth of a child (for the parent who is not taking maternity leave) | 3 working days |

Special leave is not deducted from the annual leave balance.

### 4.2 Requesting special leave
Special leave is requested through the HR assistant with the type of event and the dates. Requests within the limit in 4.1 are approved automatically. Requests for more days than the limit are sent for HR review. No notice period applies, but special leave should be taken within 30 days of the event.

## 5. Unpaid leave

### 5.1 Entitlement
Employees may take up to **20 working days** of unpaid leave per calendar year for personal reasons.

### 5.2 Approval
All unpaid leave requests require approval by the employee's manager and are therefore always sent for **HR review**. Requests that would exceed 20 working days in the calendar year are declined.

### 5.3 Effect on benefits
During unpaid leave longer than 10 working days, the sport compensation (see Benefits Policy, section 3) is paused for that month.

### 6.2 Working on a company holiday
Work on a company holiday is allowed only with prior manager approval and is compensated with another day off within 30 days.

## 7. Maternity and parental leave

### 7.1 Statutory leave
Maternity leave and parental leave to care for a child are provided according to the law. Because these cases depend on individual circumstances and documents, they are **handled by HR directly** and are not available as a request type in the HR assistant. Employees should contact HR at hr@lumora.example.
