# Remote Work Policy

## 1. Work formats

### 1.1 Office-based and remote employees
Every employee has a primary work format recorded in their profile:
- **office-based** in the **Lviv** or **Kyiv** office, or
- **fully remote** (working from anywhere in Ukraine).

### 1.2 Hybrid work for office-based employees
Office-based employees may work remotely **up to 3 days per week** by agreement with their team lead. Teams may set one common office day per week. The office is open on working days from 08:00 to 21:00.

### 1.3 Core hours
Regardless of work format, all employees should be available for meetings during core hours **11:00–16:00 Kyiv time**.

## 2. Support for remote employees

### 2.1 Coworking allowance
**Fully remote** employees may claim a coworking allowance of up to **100 USD per month** through the expense process (see Expense Reimbursement Policy). Office-based employees are not eligible because they have access to an office.

### 2.2 Home office setup
Fully remote employees receive a one-time home office allowance (see Equipment Policy, section 3).

### 2.3 Visiting an office
Remote employees are welcome to work from the Lviv or Kyiv office at any time. Travel to the office for team events is reimbursed when approved by the team lead.

## 3. Working from abroad

### 3.1 Short stays abroad
Employees (full-time and part-time) may work from abroad for **up to 30 calendar days per calendar year**. The employee must notify HR through the HR assistant or by email **at least 10 working days** before departure and confirm that the country is not under sanctions and that a stable internet connection is available.

### 3.2 Longer stays abroad
Working from abroad for more than 30 days per year has tax and legal implications and is **decided by HR individually**. Such questions should be sent to HR.

### 3.3 Contractors
Contractors decide on their place of work themselves according to their service agreement, but must comply with security requirements in the Equipment Policy, section 4.

## 4. Security when working outside the office

### 4.1 Networks and devices
Work is allowed only on company-provided devices. Public Wi-Fi may be used only with the company VPN turned on.
