# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## 1. General rules

### 1.1 Who can claim
Full-time and part-time employees can claim reimbursement of work-related expenses described in this policy. **Contractors** are reimbursed only for expenses explicitly listed in their service agreement.

### 1.2 What can be reimbursed
Reimbursable expenses include business travel, taxi rides in the cases listed below, client meals approved in advance, the coworking allowance for remote employees, sport compensation, and education expenses.

### 1.3 What is not reimbursed
Fines, personal entertainment, alcohol (except at approved client meals), and expenses without a receipt are not reimbursed.

## 2. How to submit a claim

### 2.1 Submission
Claims are submitted through the **Expenses** form in the HR portal with a photo of the receipt **within 30 calendar days** of the expense. Late claims are not accepted.

### 2.2 Approval and payment
Claims are approved by the team lead and checked by Finance. Approved claims are paid together with the **next salary payment**.

## 3. Business travel

### 3.1 Approval
All business trips require prior approval from the team lead. Travel for conferences also requires that the conference ticket is approved under the education budget.

### 3.2 Per diem and accommodation
During business travel the company pays a **per diem of 50 USD** per day for meals, plus accommodation up to **120 USD per night**. Flights and trains are booked by the office manager; employees should not buy tickets themselves unless agreed.

## 4. Taxi

### 4.1 Late work
Taxi rides home after working past **22:00** are reimbursed up to **400 UAH per ride** for office-based employees.

### 4.2 Business purposes
Taxi rides to client meetings or to the airport for a business trip are reimbursed in full.

## 5. Client meals

### 5.1 Approval and limits
Meals with clients or candidates must be approved in advance by the team lead. The limit is **40 USD per person**.
