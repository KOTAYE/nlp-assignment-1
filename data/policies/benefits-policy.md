# Benefits Policy

## 1. Overview

### 1.1 Benefits at a glance
Eligibility depends on the employment type and on whether the probation period (see Onboarding Policy, section 3) has been passed.

| Benefit | Full-time | Part-time | Contractor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Health insurance | from day 1 | after probation | not provided |
| Sport compensation | 1,000 UAH / month after probation | 500 UAH / month after probation | not provided |
| Education budget | 600 USD / year after probation | 300 USD / year after probation | not provided |
| Psychological support | 6 sessions / year | 6 sessions / year | support hotline only |
| English lessons | yes | yes | yes |

## 2. Health insurance

### 2.1 Coverage
The company pays for a health insurance policy that covers outpatient care, hospitalisation, emergency care, and dental treatment up to 10,000 UAH per year.

### 2.2 Family members
Employees may add their spouse and children to the insurance. The company covers **50%** of the cost for family members; the remaining 50% is deducted from the employee's salary.

### 2.3 How to join
Employees are enrolled automatically when they become eligible. Family members can be added within 30 days of eligibility or once per year in January. Requests go through the HR assistant or HR email.

## 3. Sport compensation

### 3.1 What is covered
Sport compensation covers gym memberships, swimming pools, sport classes, and sport club fees. Sport equipment purchases are **not** covered.

### 3.2 How to claim
The employee submits the receipt through the expense process (see Expense Reimbursement Policy, section 2) by the 25th of the month. Compensation is paid with the next salary. Unused monthly amounts do not accumulate.

### 3.3 Office partners
Employees of the Lviv and Kyiv offices may instead use partner gyms near the office at no cost, within the same monthly limit. Remote employees use receipt-based compensation only.

## 4. Education budget

### 4.1 What is covered
The education budget covers courses, certifications, conference tickets, and professional books related to the employee's current role or agreed development plan.

### 4.2 Approval
Spending over **100 USD** requires prior approval from the team lead. Conference travel costs (transport, hotel) are covered by the travel budget, not the education budget, and require separate approval (see Expense Reimbursement Policy, section 3).

### 4.3 Unused budget
The education budget is annual and does not carry over to the next year.

## 5. Psychological support

### 5.1 Sessions with a psychologist
Full-time and part-time employees can attend up to **6 sessions per year** with psychologists from the partner platform, fully paid by the company. Contractors have access to the 24/7 support hotline.

### 5.2 Confidentiality
The company does not receive any information about who used the sessions or what was discussed.

## 6. English lessons

### 6.1 Group lessons
All team members, including contractors, can join free group English lessons twice per week after a placement test.
