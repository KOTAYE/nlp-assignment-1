# Sick Leave Policy

## 1. Scope

### 1.1 Who is covered
This policy applies to **full-time and part-time employees**. **Contractors** do not receive paid sick leave from Lumora Tech; they should inform their manager about any absence according to their service agreement.

## 2. Sick days

### 2.1 Paid sick days
Lumora Tech pays **100% of salary for up to 10 sick days per calendar year**. Sick days beyond 10 in a calendar year are paid according to state social insurance rules; HR will contact the employee about the documents needed.

### 2.2 Short illness without a certificate
An employee may take up to **3 consecutive working days** of sick leave **without a medical certificate**. The employee only needs to register the sick days through the HR assistant, ideally on the first day of illness.

### 2.3 Longer illness
For an illness longer than 3 consecutive working days, a **medical certificate** (electronic sick note) is required. Such requests are registered through the HR assistant and sent for **HR review**; HR confirms the certificate and approves the request.

### 2.4 Automatic decision rules
A sick leave request is:
- **approved** automatically if it covers 3 working days or less and the employee has not exceeded 10 sick days in the calendar year including this request;
- sent for **HR review** if it covers more than 3 working days or would exceed 10 sick days in the year.

Sick leave is never declined because of short notice.

## 3. Practical rules

### 3.1 Informing the team
In addition to registering sick days, the employee should inform their team lead in the team channel as early as possible on the first day of illness.

### 3.2 Sick leave during annual leave
If an employee falls ill during annual leave and has a medical certificate, the overlapping annual leave days are returned to the balance. This requires HR processing: contact HR with the certificate.

### 3.3 Caring for a sick family member
Caring for a sick child or family member is handled under state rules with a medical certificate and is processed by HR directly.

### 3.4 Mental health days
Lumora Tech treats mental health like physical health: an employee who is not well enough to work may use sick days under the same rules. See also the Benefits Policy, section 5, for psychological support.
