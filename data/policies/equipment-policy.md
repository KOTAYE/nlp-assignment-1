# Equipment Policy

## 1. Standard equipment

### 1.1 Laptop
Every team member, including contractors, receives a company laptop: a **MacBook Pro 14"** or a **Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon**, depending on the role and personal preference.

### 1.2 Accessories
Office-based employees get a monitor, keyboard, mouse, and headset at their office desk. Remote employees receive a headset with the laptop; other accessories are covered by the home office allowance (section 3).

### 1.3 Replacement cycle
Laptops are replaced every **3 years**, or earlier if the device is broken and cannot be repaired. Requests for replacement go to IT through the IT service desk.

## 2. Care and responsibility

### 2.1 Lost or stolen equipment
Loss or theft of company equipment must be reported to IT **within 24 hours**, so that IT can lock the device remotely.

### 2.2 Damage
Accidental damage is repaired at the company's cost. Repeated damage caused by negligence may be partially charged to the employee after a review by HR.

### 2.3 Returning equipment
When leaving the company, all equipment must be returned within **5 working days** after the last working day. Remote employees return equipment by courier at the company's cost.

## 3. Home office allowance

### 3.1 Amount
**Fully remote** full-time and part-time employees receive a one-time home office allowance of **300 USD** for a monitor, chair, desk, or other accessories. The allowance is available after probation and is claimed through the expense process.

### 3.2 Office-based employees
Office-based employees working in hybrid mode are not eligible for the home office allowance.

## 4. Security requirements

### 4.1 Device rules
Company devices must have disk encryption, the company endpoint protection agent, and automatic updates enabled. Installing pirated software is forbidden. Personal devices may not be used to access company source code or customer data.
