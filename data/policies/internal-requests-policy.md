# Internal Requests Policy

## 1. Documents from HR

### 1.1 Employment certificate
Full-time and part-time employees can request an **employment certificate** (confirming position and start date). HR issues it within **2 working days**, in Ukrainian or English.

### 1.2 Income statement
An **income statement** (for a bank, visa application, or embassy) is issued within **3 working days**. Contractors receive a statement of payments under their service agreement instead, also within 3 working days.

### 1.3 How to request documents
Documents are requested through the HR portal ("Requests" section) or by email to hr@lumora.example, stating the document type, language, and purpose.

## 2. Changes to personal data

### 2.1 What to report
Employees must report changes of name, address, bank account, marital status, or number of children within **10 working days** of the change.

### 2.2 Payroll deadline
Changes to bank details reported later than **5 working days before payroll** take effect from the following month.

## 3. Referral programme

### 3.1 Referral bonus
Team members who recommend a candidate who is hired receive a referral bonus of **1,000 USD** after the new hire **passes probation**. For contractor hires, the bonus is paid after 3 months of cooperation.

### 3.2 Exclusions
HR team members and hiring managers recommending candidates for their own team are not eligible for the referral bonus.

## 4. Access requests

### 4.1 System access
Requests for access to internal systems are submitted through the IT service desk and approved by the team lead. Access to production systems requires the completed security training (see Onboarding Policy, section 2.3).

## 5. Other requests

### 5.1 Requests not covered by policies
Questions or requests that are not covered by Lumora Tech policies, such as salary reviews, promotions, relocation, or individual disputes, are handled by HR individually. The HR assistant forwards them to HR as a ticket.
